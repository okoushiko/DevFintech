01 / Why we exist

You learn to solve for x.
What about your
first paycheck?

Money is part of everyday life.
Understanding it should be, too.

Most students leave school without learning how credit works, why they overspend, or what a bank does with their money. DevFintech is a nonprofit closing that gap with free classes taught by people their own age.

We connect finance, the psychology behind our choices, and the technology changing both. All funds go toward electronics for students who cannot afford them, giving every learner the tools to succeed.

The course catalog

A starting point for every student.

Showing all 9 courses

01Personal FinanceFinance+

Budgeting, saving, and managing money day to day. Students build a budget from a real income figure, track where it leaks, and set up a savings rule they can actually keep. This is the course most chapters open with, because everything else assumes it.

02Behavioral FinanceBehavioral insights+

The psychology behind spending, investing, and money decisions in general. Loss aversion, anchoring, mental accounting, and the sunk cost trap, taught through the choices students are already making. The point isn't to name the biases, it's to catch yourself in one.

03BankingFinance+

What a bank actually does with your deposit, and why that matters to you. Covers account types, how checking and savings differ, what fees are hiding where, and how to manage income and expenses through an account instead of a shoebox.

04InvestingFinance+

An introduction to the ways money grows: stocks, index funds, bonds, and the compounding math that makes starting early matter more than picking well. Students leave able to read a ticker, explain diversification, and spot a promise that's too clean to be true.

05CodingCoding basics+

Python and HTML from nothing. Variables, loops, and functions on the Python side, structure and styling on the web side, aimed at a small finished project rather than a certificate. Finishing this course is what gets students ready for our workshops and hackathons.

06EconomicsAges 11++

Supply and demand, inflation, interest rates, and why the price of everything moved last year. We offer this course to students 11 and older so the abstractions land instead of washing over the room.

07Digital CurrenciesCoding basics+

Cryptocurrencies, the basics of blockchain, and how to keep your financial information safe online. Equal time goes to the technology and to the scams built on top of it, because the second one is what students meet first.

08LoansFinance+

An overview of loan types, what each one is used for, and the terms that come attached. Student loans, auto loans, mortgages, and credit cards, read side by side, so an APR stops being a number nobody checks.

09DebtBehavioral insights+

The different kinds of debt, how they compound, and how to manage and reduce them. Students compare payoff strategies on real balances and see what an extra payment a month actually does to a timeline.

03 / Built by students

Your school.
Your chapter.

A chapter is a club at your school, led by you, using our material. We run 60+ of them—and make it simple to start the next one.

  • 01

    Apply to lead a chapter

    Tell us your school and why you want to teach. We review applications on a rolling basis and reply within about a week.

  • 02

    Get the kit. Get ready.

    Slide decks, lesson plans and activities for all nine courses, plus a training call before your first meeting.

  • 03

    Make the club your own.

    You set the schedule and teach your members. We stay on call, share what works across chapters, and bring your students into national events.
