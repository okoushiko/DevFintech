Mission

Nobody teaches you this in school.

Most students graduate knowing how to solve for x and nothing about how a credit score works, why they overspend, or what a bank actually does with their money. We fix that with free classes taught by people their own age.

Our programs cover the fundamentals of finance, the behavior behind every money decision, and the technology reshaping both. Every dollar we raise goes toward putting laptops in the hands of students who don't have one, because a class you can't log into isn't free.

Bridging psychology, finance, and technology, for students who'd otherwise learn all three the expensive way.
Curriculum

Three things, taught properly.

Every chapter runs the same core material. It builds from why people make bad money decisions, to the tools that fix them, to the systems underneath it all.

Behavioral insights

The biases that make smart people terrible with money. Students learn to spot loss aversion, anchoring, and impulse spending in their own decisions, not just on a slide.

Coding basics

Python and HTML from zero, aimed at building something real. Students who finish the track get pulled into our workshops and hackathons, where they ship a project in a day.

Finance

Personal finance, how banks make money, investing, and crypto, explained without the jargon wall. The goal is a student who can read their own bank statement and a company's balance sheet.

How it works

We teach students through clubs.

DevFintech doesn't run one classroom, it runs 60+ of them. A chapter is a club at your school, led by you, using our material. Here's the whole path from an idea to a room full of students.

01

Apply to lead a chapter

Tell us your school and why you want to teach. We review applications on a rolling basis and reply within about a week.

02

Get the material and training

Slide decks, lesson plans, and activities for all three tracks, plus a training call so you're not walking into your first meeting cold.

03

Run your club

You set the schedule and teach your members. We stay on call, share what's working across chapters, and bring your students into national events.

Educators

Taught by students who had to learn it first.

Our instructors are high schoolers, selected through an application process and trained before they teach. Because they learned this material recently, they remember which parts were confusing.

  • Selective by application. Reviewed on academic record, leadership, and how well they explain a hard idea in plain language.
  • Trained before they teach. Every educator runs through the curriculum and a practice session before their first class.
  • Close to the material. Peers, not lecturers, the room asks more questions when the person at the front is fifteen too.
  • Backed by the network. Chapters share lesson notes, so a fix that works in one club reaches the other fifty-nine.

FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

Is it really free?

Yes. Classes, materials, and events cost students nothing. We're a nonprofit, so donations and sponsorships cover the running costs, and the rest buys laptops for students who need one.

Who can join a chapter?

Any student. Chapters are usually school clubs, so start by checking whether your school already has one. If it doesn't, you can start it.

My school doesn't have a chapter. Can I start one?

That's how most of our 60+ chapters began. Fill out the get-involved form with your school and grade, and we'll send you the application and the material.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The coding track starts from zero, and the finance and behavioral tracks don't touch code at all. Plenty of members never write a line and still get everything out of it.

Where does the money go?

Into electronics for students who can't afford them, and into running free events. Nobody at DevFintech is paid, every instructor and organizer is a volunteer.

Can adults or organizations help?

Yes. Mentors, guest speakers, sponsors, and in-kind donors all make a real difference. Email thedevfintech@gmail.com and tell us what you'd like to do.
